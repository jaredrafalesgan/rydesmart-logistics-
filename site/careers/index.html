FAQ

Driver questions

How do I apply?

Select "Apply now" on this page and complete the online driver application. It takes about 10 minutes. Our recruiting team reviews every application and contacts qualified drivers directly.

What happens after I apply?

We review your application, then contact you to talk through the position. Before you drive for us we complete the checks required for commercial drivers, including your driving record, a DOT drug screen and an FMCSA Clearinghouse query.

Can I call with questions first?

Yes. Call our main line and ask for driver recruiting.
