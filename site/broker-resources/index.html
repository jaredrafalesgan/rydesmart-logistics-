Operating authority & safety

Check our records with FMCSA

Our authority status, insurance filings and safety data are maintained by the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration — not by us. Always check the official source.

  • SAFER Company SnapshotOperating status, authority, carrier details and inspection/crash summary. Search by USDOT or MC number.
  • Licensing & Insurance (L&I)Active authority and insurance filings on record. Search by USDOT or MC number.

Identity & fraud prevention

Make sure you're dealing with us

Carrier identity fraud and double brokering are real risks. Here's how to confirm a request really comes from RydeSmart Logistics.

  • Official contact detailsWe use (678) 315-8285 and email addresses ending in @rydesmartlogistics.com. These should match our FMCSA record.
  • Payment changesWe never change remittance or factoring details by email alone. Call us to confirm any change.
  • If something looks offCall (678) 315-8285 before releasing a load.
