About us

A dependable carrier built on clear communication

RydeSmart Logistics is a freight transportation company focused on safe, on-time service and long-term relationships with brokers and shippers.

Who we are

RydeSmart Logistics is a trucking company. We haul freight for brokers and shippers who want a carrier that confirms the details, communicates on the road, and closes out every load cleanly.

We'd rather earn repeat freight from a partner than chase one-off loads. That means saying yes only when we have the right equipment and timing — and following through when we do.

Our values

How we operate

  • Safety first

    No load is worth cutting corners. Inspections, hours-of-service and careful driving come first.

  • Communication

    We tell you what's happening before you have to ask — including when plans change.

  • Accountability

    We confirm what we can do, then do what we confirmed.

  • Respect

    For our drivers, our partners, shippers' docks and everyone sharing the road.

Business approach

How we work with brokers and shippers

A simple, consistent process on every load.

  1. Before dispatchWe confirm equipment, pickup and delivery appointments, commodity, weight and rate in writing.
  2. At pickupWe let you know when we're loaded and rolling, along with any issue found at the dock.
  3. In transitStatus updates on schedule, and immediate notice if anything affects the delivery time.
  4. At deliverySigned proof of delivery and an accurate invoice, promptly.
